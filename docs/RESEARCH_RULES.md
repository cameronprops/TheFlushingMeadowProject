# Research evidence and reconstruction standard

A historical object, its identity, its location, its geometry, and its period of existence are **separate claims**; proof of one does not prove the others.

Evidence classes:
1. **DOCUMENTED_BUILT**: dated, applicable as-built plan, measured drawing or contemporary documentation of the executed version.
2. **DOCUMENTED_PROPOSED**: design proposal, competition drawing, early plan; not proof of the final structure.
3. **PHOTO_INFERRED**: reconstructed from dated imagery, with photogrammetric controls and stated uncertainty.
4. **REGISTERED_CONTEXT**: footprint or location controlled to a plan/survey; individual architecture not fully verified.
5. **UNREGISTERED_CONTEXT**: blockout placed under assumed/uncalibrated transforms.
6. **INTERPRETIVE**: authored look or detail that has no direct historical support.
7. **UNKNOWN**: evidence not yet available.

Each dimensional claim must record *source ID*, document date, measured vs inferred, units, uncertainty if estimated, and the applicable era. Null/unknown dimensions should remain unknown.

## 1939, 1940, 1964, 1965, 2026
Records refer to a specific operating state; a structure existing in 1940 is not automatically instantiated in 1939. Sparko is currently a 1940-only modeling target. City redevelopment and fair-specific grading differ by epoch.

## Review gates
- Register with source + archive asset URI
- Confirm footprint and coordinate alignment against controls
- Build massing and mark uncertain details
- Reconcile plans, sections and opening-day photographs
- Check path continuity and collision
- Validate Unreal import, lighting, performance
- Release with dated provenance and QA status
