IMPORTANT: These are renders and orthographic projections computed from the actual scene meshes.
V005 shell replacement vs preserved source shell: 49 vertices, 102 triangles, identical unordered triangle geometry. No NEW cladding cuts were made in this version.
The two green test envelopes in the views represent provisional 3.4m depth / 2.4m height / 2.4m width crossings, NOT historically approved openings or finished doorway construction.
The scene already contained a wide opening. The frame3 replacement and Helicline rail were trimmed, without certified structural redesign.
Collision surface testing is not certification of historical architecture, solid occupancy, navigation or native Unreal traversal.
