# FMP architectural research and Westinghouse schematic draft (v0.1)

The Westinghouse source dossier records what is independently supported and what is guessed. This is a standalone model at **unregistered local coordinates**, not a pavilion already inserted into the FMP historic site.

- `WESTINGHOUSE_EVIDENCE_AND_DRAFT_v001.md` — source-indexed reconstruction dossier, important measurements, archival leads and year distinctions.
- `PAVILION_RECONSTRUCTION_QUEUE_v001.csv` — eight additional candidate/priority lines (including RCA, Consumer Interests, Applied Arts and Medical), all placement-gated.
- `Westinghouse_1939_Exterior_Impression_v001.glb` — editable independent mesh objects assembled from photographed geometry; never presented as survey data.
- `Westinghouse_1939_Schematic_Plan_v001.svg` / `.png` — original technical draft indicating guessed geometry and proportions.
- `Westinghouse_1939_Schematic_Elevation_v001.svg` / `.png` — original elevation with 150 ft tower height from period source.
- `Westinghouse_1939_Draft_Manifest_v001.json` — part list, evidence statuses, parameters, source URLs, SHA-256.
- `build_westinghouse.py` — reproducible modeling script (Python 3; trimesh, numpy, matplotlib).

No historical plan scans or copyrighted archival photographs have been distributed in the package. The exact westinghouse tower dimensions are period-sourced; two hall heights and area figures are *secondary* documentary leads, while all horizontal geometry remains modeled hypothesis. **Do not build this model in place on the overall fairgrounds until the parcel is traced from 1939 plans.**
