## Usable now in an architectural reference board
1. Official guide site-map excerpt reproduced by the Hildreth Meière Association. The compact cluster joins Medicine & Public Health / Science & Education and shows neighboring RCA and AT&T. This is a **pictorial map**; no survey measurements or meter positions can be taken from it.
2. Original-looking courtyard photographs in the Meière Association collection show **curved walls**, planted lawn, pathways and the monumental *Man between the Past and the Future* artwork. This is meaningful geometry evidence for a **non-rectangular courtyard**, but without orthographic control, arc centers and radii remain unknown.

5. Southern face **Modern Medicine** artwork, **36 × 34 ft**.

7. Named neighboring faces: Washington Square (Rochambeau and Kosciuszko), southwestern/south Science & Education walls (*The Family* and *The School*).

## Interior-program evidence

- Contemporary JAMA review describes two named halls: **Hall of Man** (Oberlander Trust anatomy/physiology displays), and **Hall of Medical Science** (**28** separate exhibits). This supports an exhibit organization hypothesis but **not** a verified architectural subdivision, exhibit floor position or public route.
- NYPL's catalog lists exhibits/charts/maps/plans in Box 142 Folder 7, a 1939 proposed plan in Folder 11, and separate 1939/1940 building folders 6 and 5.
