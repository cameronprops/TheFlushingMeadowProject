# WF0028 historical images and postcard geometry analysis v001
**Status: archival links confirmed; original image files not downloaded.**

Newly located sources: an Exposition Souvenir/Grinnell 1939 linen postcard (Wikimedia Commons CC BY 2.0, Steve Shook) and a distinct Miller Art Co. postcard #124 postmarked July 3 1939. A separate CardCow listing appears to describe the Grinnell edition, so do not count it as a third independent view. The 1939 postcard description explicitly identifies a landscaped court and **spiral cascade**.

Hildreth Meière Association's period photograph/documentation identifies *Man between the Past and the Future* on a **curved courtyard-facing facade**. The existing v004 straight-wall architecture is therefore incorrect or incomplete in that part of the complex. The v016 19 m radius and v017 ~38.125 m radius curves are visual hypotheses, **not measured radii**. The photograph also shows sculptural tube/rib features in front; these must not be confused with the load-bearing architectural curved wall or the distinct spiral cascade.

Other facade constraints: the Science/Education entrance at Avenue of Patriots × Hamilton Place has a Hippocrates metal relief, approx 14x21 ft; garden-court facade *The Picnic* mural measures 14x110 ft, not necessarily the facade extent; Washington Square facade shows major rectangular masses, recesses and doorways. The 1940 pictorial map shows adjacency but has no survey metric.

**Reconstruction decision:** Separate editable features for (a) courtyard curved mural wall; (b) entrance bowed wall, conditional on photo calibration; (c) long garden mural wall; (d) Washington Square rectilinear facade; (e) spiral cascade landscape object. Fit walls only once a readable historic plan or two independently registered photographic views are available. The postcard is drawn/colored and cannot independently determine dimensions.

This source index and analytical record are archived, not the photographic originals. The Commons image is CC BY 2.0 and can be added as image bytes when the download becomes available.
