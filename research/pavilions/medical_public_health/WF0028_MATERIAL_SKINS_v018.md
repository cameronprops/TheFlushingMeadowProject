## Documented media, provisional visual parameters
- *The Family* / *The School*: gilded silhouette mosaic tesserae in a smooth, soft dull green colored cement/plaster field (contemporary 1938 description); RGB swatches remain authored hypotheses.
- *Modern Medicine*: painted using **Keim mineral paint**; exact surviving hue unverified.
- *The Picnic*: **rubber-based paint**, executed by Rambusch.
- *Primitive Man and Civilized Man*: **terra-cotta relief**.
- *Hippocrates and Dragon of Ignorance*: **aluminum and brass relief**.
- *Man between the Past and the Future*: painted mural; do not assume its paint medium or color equals the other murals.
