5. Can the Library identify any survey control sheets for Flushing Meadows Park and associated roads, bridges, rail approaches, utility structures and shoreline from 1936-1940?
6. Could the archivist suggest 1964-1965 Fair Corporation engineering/survey plans documenting the same control grid?

## FMP acceptance criteria
- Obtain at least one drawing of verified original resolution with readable scale/datum information, or a written archive response confirming the requested control data is unavailable.
- Establish at least 3 non-collinear independent coordinate correspondences plus 2 check points.
- Archive metadata: institution, collection, container, drawing number, original issue and revisions, scan resolution, URL, license/permission, exact SHA-256 of acquired file.
- Never label an inferred modern mapped feature a surveyed historical monument merely because roads appear to overlap.
- Keep purchased/restricted images in an appropriately controlled Drive folder. Git contains only permitted metadata and measurements.
